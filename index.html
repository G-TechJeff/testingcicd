A green, healthy community.

Picture a generation of young Africans who don't just talk about climate change—but take action. At Compassion Africa Initiative, we empower youth to lead the way in environmental conservation and healthy living through hands-on training in tree growing, waste management, and sustainable agriculture.

Environmental Conservation

We empower youth to lead Africa's green revolution through hands-on tree nurseries, waste management programs, and eco-education. Our initiatives combine environmental action with mental health support, creating resilient young stewards for sustainable development.

Advocating for

Public Health

More details..

We promote community health through education, preventive care initiatives, and improved healthcare access. Our programs address critical health disparities while fostering holistic well-being in underserved African communities.

A sustainable, healthy community.

Why So Serious?

Compassion Africa Initiative is a youth led non-profit organization nurturing a young African generation of environmental stewards and health advocates.
By partnering with learning institutions, we establish tree nurseries, promote solid waste management, and foster public health, to inspire sustainable living. Together, we’re growing a movement where communities conserve their environment and prioritize health, one tree and one life at a time. Join us in planting the seeds of change!

A Community Day.

Compassion Africa Initiative volunteers, in partnership with Rotary Club of Juja, The Ruiru Sports Club and Finsco Africa, brought together volunteers, youth environmental stewards, and guests for a transformative Community Day at Ruiru Sports Club.
Together, we planted fruit and indigenous trees across the grounds, fostering not just a greener future but also lasting friendships among the youth. Every seedling symbolizes hope, unity, and our commitment to a sustainable planet.
